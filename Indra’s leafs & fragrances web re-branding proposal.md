### Proposed business model

#### Aim:

1. Client wants to boost their business by making more prominent online presence.
2. Client wants to sell online also as till now they have sold their products only through offline mode.
3. Client wants to reach more users/ consumers through their website and social profiles.
4. Client wants a re-branding of the website by scraping the existing one.
5. Client wants to rank up their current website page rank in search engines.
6. Client wants a dedicated and consistent social promotion particularly in Facebook and Instagram on regular basis.
7. Client is open to choose a new appropriate domain name in order to bring more conversion.

### Proposed development platform for the website

Full stack React web application with React and Mongo DB and with Stripe for managing online payments.
