## Highlights of the Proposal

### 1. Technology & Architecture Strategy
- **Next.js SSR vs. MERN React SPA:** Detailed how SSR in Next.js resolves Google crawler indexation constraints, addressing the client's core goal to "rank up current website page rank in search engines" (Aim 5).
- **Architecture Diagram:** Provided a Mermaid visualization of Next.js + Vercel + Node.js + MongoDB Atlas.

### 2. Payment Gateway Solution
- **Stripe Compliance in India:** Addressed the invite-only and business structure requirements for Stripe India in 2026.
- **Razorpay Alternative:** Recommended Razorpay for a friction-free domestic launch with 2% flat fees (UPI, Cards, Netbanking) and zero AMC/setup costs.

### 3. Financial Analysis
- Provided a consolidated table summarizing one-time development and social media setup costs vs. monthly recurring cloud/DB hosting and flexible marketing budgets in Indian Rupees (INR).

### 4. Project Roadmap
- Mapped out a 12-week development timeline using a Mermaid Gantt Chart.
- Included an adjusted **18–24 week timeline** analysis specifically tailored for a single junior developer who has just learned the MERN stack.

### 5. Budget-Constrained Options
- Analyzed two options to lower costs:
  - **Option A (WordPress/WooCommerce Re-vamp):** Lowers dev cost to ₹30k–₹50k and timeline to 3–4 weeks by updating their current platform instead of a full rewrite.
  - **Option B (MERN MVP):** Cuts custom admin dashboard components using free headless CMS systems (Strapi/Sanity) and free cloud tiers, reducing dev cost to ₹70k–₹100k.
